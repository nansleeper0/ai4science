# Источник M2024

[источник] Alexander A. Mushtukov, Albert Weng, Sergey S. Tsygankov, Ilya A. Mereminskiy, "Flickering pulsations in bright X-ray pulsars: the evidence of gravitationally lensed and eclipsed accretion column", Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society, vol. 530, issue 3, pp. 3051–3058, 2024 (препринт 2404.04137v1). Модель затмения и гравитационного линзирования аккреционной колонны в суперкритическом режиме, проверенная на V 0332+53.
[источник] Аннотация: It is expected that the extreme mass accretion rate onto strongly magnetized neutron stars results in the appearance of accretion columns above the stellar surface. For a distant observer, rotation of a star results in periodic variations of X-ray flux. Because the mass accretion rate fluctuates around the average value, the pulse profiles are not stable and demonstrate fluctuations as well. In the case of bright X-ray pulsars, however, pulse fluctuations are not solely attributed to variations in the mass accretion rate.
[порождено] Зачем проекту: первоисточник модели column-eclipse связи периодической и апериодической переменности.
